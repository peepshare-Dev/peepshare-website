Community Guidelines

Last Edited: August 31, 2026

To ensure that PEEP SHARE remains a safe, appropriate, and constructive Public Community,
or “Community”, for communication, exchange of opinions, sharing of information, participation in
public communities, and use of various services on the platform, we have prepared these
“Community Guidelines” to set out the rules and standards regarding content, conduct, and use of services that users are expected to follow. These Guidelines apply to chats, posts, comments, content sharing, use of Public Community, interactions with official accounts, bots, partner services, Marketplace, Peep Shop, or any other features of PEEP SHARE.

These Community Guidelines form part of the Terms of Service of PEEP SHARE. Therefore, you, as a user or service user, should read these Guidelines together with the Terms of Service and any other relevant notices or policies to ensure that your use of the platform, content, and conduct comply with our community standards and do not expose you to the risk of being reported, restricted, suspended, or having your account terminated.

PURPOSE OF THE COMMUNITY

  1. The PEEP SHARE Community is established as a space for communication, exchange of opinions, sharing of information, building relationships, and accessing services or benefits within the platform in an appropriate, safe, and respectful manner.

  2. You should use PEEP SHARE responsibly and must not use the platform in any manner that causes damage, disturbance, infringement of rights, harassment, deception, impersonation, or any act that violates the law, the Terms of Service, or these Community Guidelines, unless otherwise specified in these Guidelines.

PERMITTED CONDUCT

  1. Creating posts, commenting, sharing information, images, videos, files, or other content that is appropriate, lawful, and does not infringe the rights of others.

  2. Chatting, exchanging opinions, asking questions, giving advice, or sharing experiences using polite and constructive language, while respecting the differences of other users.

  3. Creating or joining Public Community, public rooms, group chats, or other community spaces in accordance with the purpose of such spaces and the rules set by system administrators or community moderators.

  4. Using AI Assistant, bots, official accounts, partner services, Marketplace, Peep Shop, or other services connected to PEEP SHARE in accordance with the purpose of such services, and not using them for deception, impersonation, infringement of rights, or any unlawful activity.

  5. Reporting content, conduct, or accounts that may violate these Community Guidelines, the Terms of Service, or applicable laws through the channels designated by us.

STRICTLY PROHIBITED CONDUCT

  1. Posting, sending, sharing, uploading, or publishing content that is unlawful, supports unlawful activity, or encourages others to engage in unlawful activity.

  2. Harassment, threats, bullying, insults, abusive language, offensive conduct, or any act that causes other users to suffer distress, damage, or feel unsafe.

  3. Publishing content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or attacks against any individual or group based on race, religion, gender, age, disability, opinion, or any other protected status under applicable law.

  4. Publishing another person’s personal data without authorization, such as real name, telephone number, address, email address, national identification number, photographs, personal documents, financial information, health information, location data, or any other information that may cause damage to such person.

  5. Impersonating another person, organization, agency, official account, merchant, business partner, partner, or system administrator, including creating fake accounts to deceive, mislead, or cause damage to others.

  6. Fraud, scams, phishing, spam, sending malicious links, soliciting money transfers, selling counterfeit goods or services, or falsely claiming promotions, benefits, or activities of PEEP SHARE without authorization.

  7. Unauthorized hidden marketing, advertising, promotion of goods, services, external channels, or campaigns in areas where such activities are not permitted, or in a manner that disturbs other users.

  8. Publishing obscene content, sexual content, sexually exploitative content, inappropriate content involving minors, or content that is contrary to public order or good morals.

  9. Publishing violent, cruel, shocking, self-harm-promoting, harm-promoting, or dangerous content.

  10. Infringing intellectual property rights, image rights, reputation, privacy, or any other rights of third parties, such as using images, videos, music, text, designs, trademarks, or other content without authorization.

  11. Using AI Assistant, bots, or automated features in an inappropriate manner, such as creating deceptive content, impersonating others, creating deepfakes, generating messages or media that may mislead others, or using AI-generated outputs without appropriate review.

  12. Any act that affects the system, security, or experience of other users, such as attempting to access the system without authorization, disrupting the system, using unauthorized bots or scripts, scraping or collecting large amounts of user data, or any other act that may pose a threat to the platform.

  13. Any act that violates the Terms of Service, Privacy Notice, other policies of PEEP SHARE, or applicable laws.

REPORTING INAPPROPRIATE CONTENT

  1. Users may report content, comments, messages, user accounts, chat rooms, Public Community, merchants, official accounts, or partner services that may violate these Community Guidelines, the Terms of Service, or applicable laws through the channels designated by us.

  2. When submitting a report, users should provide sufficient information for review, such as the reason for the report, details of the relevant content or conduct, screenshots, links, or any other supporting information that may be useful for our assessment. We will review the report based on the information received, the nature of the content, the context of use, the seriousness of
    the conduct, and applicable laws.

MODERATOR RESPONSE MEASURES

  1. We provide system administrators or moderators who have the right to review and take appropriate action. Such action may include any of the following:

    1. Warning users or providing guidance to correct conduct or content;

    2. Restricting visibility, reducing reach, hiding, deleting, or suspending the publication of content, comments, posts, files, chat rooms, or related information;

    3. Restricting access to certain features, such as posting, commenting, sending messages, creating rooms, joining Public Community, using official accounts, bots, Marketplace, Peep Shop, or other related services;

    4. Temporarily suspending or permanently suspending accounts in cases of repeated violations, serious violations, or where necessary to prevent damage to other users, the platform, or third parties;

    5. Notifying competent authorities or taking legal action if the conduct may constitute a violation of law, affect security, or cause damage to us, other users, or third parties.

  2. Moderator actions may vary depending on the nature of the conduct, seriousness, frequency, user history, impact, and applicable legal requirements. We may act without prior notice where necessary to prevent damage or maintain the security of the platform.

COMPLAINT CHANNEL

  1. If you believe that an action taken by the system administrator or moderator is incorrect, unfair, or that there is additional information that should be considered, you may submit a request for review through the channel or form designated by PEEP SHARE.

  2. In submitting a request, you should provide details of the account, content, or action that you wish to be reviewed, the reason for the request, and any additional relevant information or evidence. We will review the request as appropriate within a reasonable period. Our decision shall be final, unless otherwise required by applicable law.
