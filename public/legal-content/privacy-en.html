WHAT RIGHTS DO YOU HAVE AS DATA SUBJECT

  1. We respect your individual rights and provide you with the opportunity to exercise the following rights under Personal Data Protection Laws:

    1. Right to Withdraw Consent:

If you have consented to the processing of personal data, whether before or after Personal Data Protection Laws came into force, you may withdraw your consent at any time while we retain your personal data. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal. This right may be restricted by law, where consent cannot by its nature be withdrawn, or where a contract between you and us continues to provide you with benefits. Withdrawal may prevent us from fulfilling some or all of the purposes set out in this document, may affect your use of our platform, and may prevent you from receiving certain benefits in full. For your benefit, we recommend that you understand and ask about the consequences before exercising your right to withdraw consent.

  1. Right of Access:

You have the right to request access to your personal data under our responsibility, request a copy of that data, and ask us to disclose how we obtained it, unless we are entitled to refuse your request under applicable law or a court order, or where granting the request may adversely affect the rights and freedoms of another person.

  1. Right to Data Portability:

You have the right to request the transfer of your personal data where we have arranged that data in a format readable or commonly usable by automated tools or devices and the data can be used or disclosed by automated means. You may request that we transmit or transfer the data in that format to another data controller where technically feasible and request to receive the data that we transmit or transfer directly to another data controller, unless doing so is technically impossible.

  1. Right to Object:

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data at any time where the processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, or those of another person or legal entity, and does not exceed what you could reasonably expect; is carried out in the public interest; is for marketing purposes; or is for scientific research or statistical purposes. If you object, we may continue processing where we can demonstrate compelling lawful grounds that override your fundamental rights, or where the processing is necessary for the establishment, compliance with, exercise, or defense of legal claims, as applicable.

  1. Right to Erasure:

You have the right to request that your personal data be erased or destroyed, or rendered anonymous, if you believe it has been processed unlawfully under applicable laws, if we no longer need to retain it for the purposes set out in this Privacy Notice, or where we can comply with your exercise of the right to withdraw consent or the right to object described above.

  1. Right to Restriction Processing:

You have the right to request the temporary restriction of the use of your personal data while we verify a request to rectify personal data or an objection you have made, or where we are otherwise required to erase or destroy your personal data under applicable law because it is no longer necessary, but you request restriction of its use instead.

  1. Right to Rectification:

If you believe that your personal data is inaccurate, you may notify us so that we can correct or amend it, or supplement it so that it is accurate, up to date, complete, and not misleading. If you request rectification of image-related data, we will correct only the entries relating to your image to the extent necessary for our legitimate purposes. Where complying with your request results in costs, we may charge those costs. If we have grounds to refuse your request, we will keep a record of the refusal and the reasons for it.

  1. Right to Lodge a Complaint:

  2. You have the right to lodge a complaint with us through our designated channels if you believe that the processing of personal data violates or fails to comply with applicable laws.

  1. We may nevertheless refuse your exercise of any of the rights above in accordance with the criteria and conditions prescribed by Personal Data Protection Laws or other applicable laws. If we refuse your request, we will clearly inform you of the reasons for the refusal.
