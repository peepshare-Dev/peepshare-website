(FAQs)

Your Questions, Answered

Helping you understand our process and offerings at Agero.

Why's Agero instead of a full-time designer?+

Agero gives you senior-level design without the overhead of hiring — no onboarding, benefits, or long-term commitment. Pause or cancel anytime.

How do I request a design?+

Send us a brief through the contact form or your dashboard. We'll scope it, confirm the timeline, and get started within 1 business day.

Speed of design delivery?+

Pretty quick! Most designs are delivered in 2–3 business days. We prioritize quality without slowing you down.

What if I don't like the design?+

We revise until you're happy. Every plan includes unlimited revisions within the scope of your request.

Are there any refunds?+

Work starts immediately, so we don't refund completed deliverables — but you can pause or cancel before your next billing cycle.

What's the Agero progress tracking like?+

You'll get a shared board to track every request — queued, in progress, delivered — updated in real time.
